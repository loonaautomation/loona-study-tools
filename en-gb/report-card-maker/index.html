Report Card Maker — Printable Report Cards in Minutes

In short: enter the student's details and marks by subject, and this maker calculates the totals, the overall percentage and the grade for you. Add class position, attendance and teacher remarks, watch the A4 report card update live as you type, then print it or save it in your browser — no spreadsheet, no sign-up, and nothing ever leaves your device.

Making report cards by hand or in a spreadsheet takes hours, and one wrong total can quietly change a student's grade. This maker does the arithmetic as you type, applies your own school's grading scale, and lays everything out as a clean, professional report card that is ready to print and sign.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the school name, student name, class and section, roll number and the exam or term name. Everything you type appears on the card preview at once.
  2. Add or remove subject rows, then type each subject's total marks and the marks obtained. The percentage for every row, the totals and the overall percentage are calculated automatically as you type.
  3. Check the grading scale. The five cut-off boxes start at the common A+ 90, A 80, B 70, C 60 and D 50 scale — change any of them if your school grades differently, and the grade updates instantly.
  4. Add the class position if your school records it, and the attendance figures if you want them on the card. The attendance percentage appears only when both the days present and the total school days are filled in.
  5. Tap a quick-pick remark to drop it into the remarks box, or write your own. You can edit a quick-pick remark after tapping it.
  6. Check the live preview, then press Print report card — only the card prints, on A4. To keep a card for later, give it a name in the save box and press Save report card; it will be waiting in the saved list on this device.

How It Works

Every calculation happens in your browser, the moment you type. For each subject, the row percentage is the marks obtained divided by the total marks, multiplied by 100. The overall percentage is the sum of all marks obtained divided by the sum of all total marks, multiplied by 100 and rounded to two decimal places — so a subject with more marks counts for more, exactly as it should. The grade is then looked up in your grading scale from the top down: the first cut-off the percentage reaches is the grade awarded, which is why 90.00% earns an A+ on the default scale while 89.99% earns an A. Attendance works the same way: days present divided by total school days, shown as a percentage only when both numbers are present.

Nothing is sent to a server. The form, the live preview and the print layout are all produced on your device, and a card is only stored when you press Save — in your browser's local storage, under the name you give it. The print button uses a dedicated print stylesheet that hides the form, the navigation and everything else on the page, so the printer receives only the report card itself, sized for A4 paper with signature lines for the class teacher and the principal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Report Card Maker really free?

Yes. You can make, save and print as many report cards as you like, with no sign-up, no watermark and no limit. The tool is part of Loona Study Tools and is supported by unobtrusive advertising.

Can I change the grading scale to match my school?

Yes — that is what the five cut-off boxes are for. Type your school's minimum percentage for A+, A, B, C and D, and every grade is recalculated at once. Anything below your D cut-off is graded F. The scale you set is stored with the card when you save it.

Where are student names and marks stored? Is the data private?

Only on your device. Typing in the form stores nothing anywhere; pressing Save keeps the card in your own browser's local storage so you can reopen it later. No name, mark or remark is ever uploaded, sent to Loona or shared with anyone, and deleting a saved card removes it completely.

How do I print a card, or save it as a PDF?

Press the Print report card button. A print stylesheet hides the form and the rest of the page, so only the report card is printed, laid out for A4. In the print dialog you can choose your printer, or choose “Save as PDF” to keep a digital copy instead.

Our subjects do not all have the same total marks. Does that matter?

No. Every subject row has its own total marks box, so a 50-mark test and a 100-mark paper can sit on the same card. The overall percentage is weighted correctly because it is calculated from the summed marks obtained and the summed maximum marks, not by averaging the row percentages.

Can I reuse a saved report card next term?

Yes. Open the saved card from the saved list, change the exam or term name and the new marks, and save it again under a new name. The student details, subjects and grading scale are already filled in, so a new term's card takes a fraction of the time.
