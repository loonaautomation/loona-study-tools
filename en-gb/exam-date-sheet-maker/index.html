Exam Date Sheet Maker — A Clean, Print-Ready Exam Schedule

In short: type your school name, class and exam name, add one row per paper — date, subject, time and room — and this maker fills in the weekday automatically, keeps the papers in date order, and prints a clean A4 date sheet you can pin up or hand out.

A date sheet is the one exam document every student, parent and teacher checks again and again — so it has to be clear, correct and easy to print. This free maker builds it for you: weekdays are worked out from the dates (never typed by hand, so they cannot be wrong), papers stay in date order, and the printed sheet is a tidy A4 table with your school header and your instructions to students underneath.

🔒 Private by design: your date sheet is built on this device only. It is saved only in this browser (so you can return and edit it) and is never uploaded to a server.

Exam papers

Your work is auto-saved in this browser as you type, so you can close the page and come back to it. Use Save / Load / Clear to manage the saved copy yourself.

How to Use It

  1. Fill in the header: your school name, the class or grade, the exam name (for example “Annual Exam 2027”) and the session or year. These appear at the top of the printed sheet.
  2. Add one row per paper: pick the date — the weekday fills in automatically — then type the subject, the paper start and end times, and an optional room or invigilator note.
  3. Arrange the papers: with auto-sort on, papers arrange themselves in date order. Switch it off if you need a manual order, and use the up and down buttons, Duplicate and Remove to adjust rows.
  4. Write the instructions: add the notes students must follow — reporting time, what to bring, what is not allowed. They print under the table.
  5. Print or save as PDF: press Print / Save as PDF. Only the date sheet itself prints, on A4 — the editor, buttons and menus stay off the page.

How It Works

The weekday for each paper is calculated from the date you pick, using the calendar on your own device — it is never typed in, so a date sheet can never show the wrong day next to a date. When auto-sort is on, the rows are ordered by date every time you change a date; papers without a date stay at the end. Everything runs in your browser: your sheet is auto-saved to this browser’s local storage as you work, and nothing is sent to a server. The print view rebuilds your entries as a clean table — Date, Day, Subject, Time and Room/Note — under your school header, with your instructions underneath.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the weekday really automatic?

Yes. Pick a date and the correct weekday appears next to it straight away, worked out from that date. Change the date and the weekday updates with it, so the day and the date can never disagree.

Can I turn the date sorting off?

Yes — untick “Auto-sort papers by date” and the rows stay in exactly the order you leave them. Use the up and down buttons on each row to arrange them by hand.

Is my date sheet saved?

Yes, in this browser only. The maker auto-saves as you type, and the Save, Load saved sheet and Clear all buttons let you manage that saved copy yourself. Nothing is uploaded, and clearing the saved copy removes it from this browser.

What does the printed sheet look like?

A clean A4 sheet: your school name as the heading, the exam, class and session line under it, a table with Date, Day, Subject, Time and Room/Note columns, and your instructions to students at the bottom. Only the sheet prints — none of the editing controls appear on paper.

Can I add two papers on the same day?

Yes — add as many rows as you need, including morning and evening papers on the same date. Give each its own start and end time and, if needed, a different room.
