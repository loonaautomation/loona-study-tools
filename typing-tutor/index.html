Learn-to-Type Tutor — Real Lessons, Not Just a Test

In short: This tutor teaches you to type, step by step: twelve short lessons take you from the home row to the full keyboard — capitals and numbers included — while the on-screen keyboard lights up every next key. Finish a lesson with 85% accuracy or better to unlock the next one.

A typing test only measures you; it never teaches you. This tutor is the other half: a patient course that introduces a few keys at a time, drills them until they feel easy, tracks which keys you personally miss, and only moves on when you are ready. There is also a gentle Kids Mode with shorter lessons and star rewards. When you want to measure your speed afterwards, take the Typing Test on Loona Text Tools — learn here first.

How to Use It

  1. Pick a lesson — start with Lesson 1 (the home row). Locked lessons open one by one as you pass the ones before them.
  2. Type the glowing letters — the on-screen keyboard lights up the exact key to press next, so your fingers learn where every key lives.
  3. A wrong key does not move you on — press the right key to continue. Mistakes count in your accuracy and are remembered in your Key Accuracy panel.
  4. Finish with 85% accuracy or better (75% in Kids Mode) to unlock the next lesson, and see your WPM and accuracy for the lesson.
  5. Come back daily — ten minutes of lessons a day builds real touch-typing faster than one long session.

How It Works

The course follows the classic touch-typing order used by typing schools: the home row first (your fingers' home base), then the top row, then the bottom row, then capitals with Shift, and finally numbers and full sentences. Each lesson adds only a few new keys and mixes them with keys you already know, which is exactly how muscle memory forms. The tutor tracks every keystroke per key, so the Key Accuracy panel can show your personal weak keys — the ones worth an extra round of practice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a typing test?

A test gives you a text and a score — it assumes you can already type. This tutor teaches: it introduces two keys at a time, drills them, shows the next key on an on-screen keyboard, tracks your personal problem keys, and unlocks lessons in order. Learn here first; then measure yourself with the Typing Test on Loona Text Tools whenever you feel ready.

What is Kids Mode?

A shorter, gentler path for children: six mini-lessons with less text per lesson, big friendly targets and star rewards. Kids unlock the next lesson at 75% accuracy instead of 85%, because encouragement beats pressure at that age.

How are WPM and accuracy calculated?

Accuracy is correct keystrokes divided by all keystrokes, including mistakes. WPM uses the standard typing convention of 5 characters per word, counting only correctly typed characters, over the time from your first keystroke to the last one. A wrong key never moves you forward — you press the right key to continue, exactly like real touch-typing practice.

Is my progress saved?

Yes — on this device only. Your unlocked lessons, best scores and per-key accuracy are stored in your browser's local storage, never on a server, and are never shared with anyone. Use Reset all progress to wipe them.
