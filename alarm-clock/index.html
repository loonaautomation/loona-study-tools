How to Use It

  1. Pick a time: use the alarm time field to choose when the alarm should ring. If that time has already passed today, the alarm is set for tomorrow automatically.
  2. Add a label and a sound: give the alarm a name like “Wake up” or “Lesson ends”, then choose one of four sounds — Classic Beep, Gentle Chime, Digital Alarm or Soft Bell. Press Test sound to hear it first.
  3. Press Add Alarm: the alarm appears in Your Alarms with its own on/off switch. Add as many as you like — a wake-up alarm, a study-break alarm and a lesson-end alarm can all live side by side.
  4. Keep this tab open: the alarm rings from this tab, so leave the page open (you can use other tabs). Use Fullscreen for a big bedside or classroom display, and turn on “Keep screen awake” so the display stays lit where your browser supports it.
  5. When it rings: the ringing panel appears, the sound loops and the page title flashes. Press Snooze 5 min or Snooze 10 min to be reminded again shortly, or Dismiss to stop it. A dismissed alarm switches itself off and rolls to the same time tomorrow, ready to be switched on again.
  6. Your alarms are remembered: alarms and your 12/24-hour choice are saved in this browser, so they are still here when you reopen the page on the same device.

How It Works

The live clock is drawn from your device's own clock and refreshed four times a second, so it always shows your real local time. Each alarm stores the exact moment it should next ring; the page checks that moment against the device clock several times a second, which means an alarm cannot drift even if the tab was busy. The four sounds are synthesised live with the Web Audio API — oscillators shaped into beeps, chimes and bells — so there are no audio files to download and the sounds work offline once the page has loaded. Your alarms and settings are kept in your browser's local storage on this device only; nothing is sent to a server. Where the browser offers the Screen Wake Lock API, the “Keep screen awake” button asks it to stop the screen sleeping, and the lock is re-requested automatically when you return to the tab.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will the alarm ring if I close the tab?

No. This is a web page, not an installed app, so it can only ring while the tab is open — keep this tab open and the alarm will ring from this tab at the set time. If you need an alarm after closing everything, use your phone's built-in clock app as a backup.

Why is there no sound?

Check three things: the device is not muted or in Do Not Disturb, the browser tab is not muted (right-click the tab to check), and you have interacted with the page at least once — browsers only allow sound after a tap or click, which is why pressing Add Alarm or Test sound first matters. Then press Test sound to confirm audio works before relying on the alarm.

Can I set more than one alarm?

Yes — as many as you like. Each alarm has its own time, label, sound and on/off switch, so you can keep a whole day of reminders and switch each one on or off without deleting it.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The page works on phones, tablets and computers, the big display is easy to read at a distance, and Fullscreen mode turns it into a bedside-style clock. Keep the tab open and, where your browser supports it, turn on “Keep screen awake” so the display does not sleep. Charging the phone overnight is a good idea for a morning alarm.

Are my alarms saved?

Yes — in this browser, on this device. Your alarms and your 12/24-hour preference are stored in local storage and are still there when you reopen the page. They are not synced to other devices or browsers, and clearing the browser's site data will remove them.
