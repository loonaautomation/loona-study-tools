Grade 2 Word Problems Practice

Skill: Two-digit word problems (join, separate, compare). A worked example first, then 6 practice questions with answers you can reveal — or print the page for a worksheet with the answer key hidden until you choose to check.
🖨️ Print tip: answers stay hidden on paper until revealed on screen — print first for a clean worksheet, then check together on screen.

Worked example

A class collected 67 bottle caps. Another class collected 34. How many more did the first class collect?

  1. "How many more" is a comparison — subtract.
  2. 67 − 34 = 33.

Answer: 33 more bottle caps

Practice questions

1. Zainab read 26 pages on Monday and 31 on Tuesday. Total pages? 57

2. Zainab read 45 pages on Monday and 28 on Tuesday. Total pages? 73

3. A shop had 84 eggs and sold 37. How many are left? 47

4. A shop had 63 eggs and sold 29. How many are left? 34

5. Team A scored 57 points, Team B scored 34. Difference? 23

6. Team A scored 72 points, Team B scored 48. Difference? 24

How it works

Sort the story: total unknown (add), part unknown (subtract), or comparing two amounts (subtract).

The common mistake

Key-word hunting ("more" always means add) — "how many more" actually subtracts.

Top tip

Draw a bar model: one long bar, one short bar, the gap is the answer.
